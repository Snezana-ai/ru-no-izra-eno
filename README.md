# Ručno Izrađeno

Build a Web Application for Handmade Products Marketplace

1. Project Overview

Create a modern, responsive web application that connects people who create, own, or sell handmade and unique products with customers who want to discover and buy these products.

The platform is designed especially for:

hobby handmade creators

small handmade businesses and entrepreneurs

people who want to sell inherited or vintage handmade items

customers looking for unique and authentic products

souvenir shops and similar businesses that may want to purchase handmade products

The main purpose of the platform is to make it easier for small handmade producers and owners of unique handmade items to present their products online and connect directly with potential buyers.

This is an MVP/prototype for a university project, so prioritize clarity, usability, attractive design, and realistic core functionality over unnecessary complexity.

2. Main Concept

The platform should work as a marketplace for handmade and unique products.

A seller can:

create an account

create a personal profile

upload handmade products

add product photos

enter the product name

add a description

set a price

select a category

receive messages from interested customers

communicate with customers

arrange payment and delivery with the customer

A buyer can:

create an account

browse products

search for products

filter products by category and price

open a product page

see the seller's profile

contact the seller

express interest in buying the product

arrange payment and delivery with the seller

12. Purchase and Delivery Concept

The platform should support two possible ways of completing a transaction.

Option 1 – Direct arrangement

Buyer and seller communicate through the platform and agree on:

payment method

delivery

courier service

pickup

Option 2 – Online payment concept

The platform should have a visible option for online payment, but for this MVP it can be represented as a prototype interface rather than a fully integrated payment gateway.

Do not implement real financial transactions unless necessary.

The MVP should demonstrate how the process would work.

After an agreement, the seller sends the product to the buyer's address using a courier service.

13. Business Model

Implement the following pricing model for sellers.

First 5 products are FREE

Every seller can publish their first 5 products completely free of charge.

After the seller reaches 5 published products:

100 RSD / month for every additional 10 products

For example:

Products 1–5 → FREE

Products 6–15 → 100 RSD/month

Products 16–25 → another 100 RSD/month

Products 26–35 → another 100 RSD/month

Display the seller's current product count and their current plan/status.

Create a simple pricing/usage section in the seller dashboard.

For the MVP, the payment itself can be simulated.

19. Visual Design

The design should feel:

authentic

elegant

modern

trustworthy

simple

The visual identity should communicate craftsmanship, creativity and authenticity.

Use:

warm neutral backgrounds

elegant typography

large high-quality product photography

generous spacing

rounded but not overly playful UI elements

subtle shadows

clean cards

The design should be inspired by the feeling of traditional handmade crafts while still looking like a modern digital platform.

Use the accent color approximately similar to:

#A6533C

Use it mainly for:

primary buttons

links

important UI elements

selected states

Do not use the accent color everywhere.

20. Typography

27. MVP Priorities

This is an MVP for a university project.

Prioritize these features first:

User registration/login

User profiles

Product creation

Product browsing

Search and filtering

Messaging

Seller dashboard

Do not spend development time on advanced features that are not necessary for the MVP.

Real payment processing, complex logistics integration, advanced recommendation algorithms and sophisticated analytics can remain future features.

29. Important UX Principle

The application should not feel crowded.

Use plenty of white/neutral space.

Prioritize images and short pieces of information.

Avoid displaying large amounts of text at once.

Product cards should be simple.

Important actions should be obvious.

The user should understand what the platform does within a few seconds of opening the homepage.

30. Final Goal

Build a polished, realistic MVP of a Serbian handmade marketplace platform.

The result should look like a real startup product that could be demonstrated to potential users, while remaining simple enough to explain as an MVP in a university project.

The application should demonstrate the complete basic journey:

Register → create profile → publish handmade product → discover products → contact seller → arrange purchase/delivery.

Make the interface visually attractive, coherent, modern and easy to use.

Do not create unnecessary complexity. Focus on a functional and convincing MVP.
